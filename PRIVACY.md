UltiObserver Privacy Policy
===========================

Effective date: May 21, 2026

UltiObserver is an Android app by Mike Jarvis for managing Ultimate observer
game information such as time, score, cards, timeouts, pull violations, and
game summaries.

Data stored by the app
----------------------

UltiObserver stores the information needed to run and review games on your
device. This may include:

* Your observer profile name and avatar preference.
* App settings, including timing cue sound and vibration preferences.
* Current game setup and state, including team names, tournament name, rules,
  score, timeouts, pull direction, cards, technical fouls, pull violations,
  time violations, timestamps, and event log entries.
* Archived games and game summaries.

This information is stored locally in the app's private storage on your device.

Data collection and sharing
---------------------------

UltiObserver does not have ads, analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, or user
accounts. The app does not send your game data to Mike Jarvis or to any server
operated by UltiObserver.

If you choose to share a game summary, UltiObserver uses Android's normal share
sheet. The summary is sent only to the app or person you choose in that share
flow.

Android backup
--------------

Android may include UltiObserver's app-private data in its normal device backup
system, depending on your phone, Android version, Google account, and backup
settings. Those backups are controlled by Android and Google account backup
settings, not by UltiObserver.

Permissions
-----------

UltiObserver requests vibration permission so it can provide optional haptic
timing cues. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, or
internet permission.

Deleting your data
------------------

You can delete UltiObserver's local data by clearing the app's storage in Android
settings or uninstalling the app. Android backup behavior is controlled by your
device and Google account backup settings.

Children
--------

UltiObserver is not directed to children under 13.
