Privacy
=======

UltiObserver does not have ads, in-app purchases, or user accounts. It does not
intentionally send your game data to Mike Jarvis or to any server operated by
UltiObserver.

The app stores your profile, settings, current game, and archived games locally
on your device. Android may include this app data in normal device backup,
depending on your phone and Google account settings.

Release builds use Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase to
collect crash reports and diagnostic information. Those reports are intended to
help diagnose stability problems, not to collect game setup, scores, cards,
event logs, archived games, profile names, or other game-management content.

If you share a game summary, Android's share sheet sends the summary only to
the app or person you choose.

For the formal policy, read the `UltiObserver privacy policy`_.

.. _UltiObserver privacy policy: https://github.com/rmjarvis/UltiObserver/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
