Installation
============

UltiObserver is currently in Google Play beta testing, so it's not available yet on
the regular Google Play menu. To install it, you need to do the following:

1. Join the `UltiObserver testers Google Group`_. Make sure to use the same email address
   you use with Google Play.
2. Open the `Google Play testing page`_ and click "Become a tester".
3. Go to the `UltiObserver Google Play page`_ and click "Install".

If you have trouble joining or installing, ping @Mike Jarvis on the observer
Discord, or send an email to mikejarvis17 [at] gmail [dot] com.

.. _UltiObserver testers Google Group: https://groups.google.com/g/ultiobserver-testers
.. _Google Play testing page: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/rmjarvis.ultiobserver
.. _UltiObserver Google Play page: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=rmjarvis.ultiobserver

Updating UltiObserver
---------------------

If you already have an earlier version installed, you do not need to uninstall it.
Going to the `UltiObserver Google Play page`_ should give you an option to update it.
In fact, if automatic updates are enabled, it should just update itself without
you needing to do anything.

.. note::

   You can also access the Google Play link from within the app.
   Open UltiObserver's **About** page (the small circled i at the upper left
   of the home page) and tap **Check for updates**.

Installing on a Wear OS Watch
-----------------------------

UltiObserver has an optional companion app for Wear OS smart watches. If you have
one, we highly recommend also installing UltiObserver on your watch.
Install the phone app first using the instructions above. Then:

1. Open the `UltiObserver Google Play page`_ in a web browser. (Not the Google Play app.
   For some reason the next step only seems to be available on the web page.)
   It should show that you have already installed UltiObserver on your phone.
2. Select **Install on more devices**, choose your watch, then click **Install**.
   It may take a few minutes for it to install on the watch.

Once the installation is complete, you should be able to open UltiObserver on the
watch.
See `Wear OS Companion App` for usage instructions and `Watch Troubleshooting` if it
does not connect.

If Google Play does not offer the watch installation, check the account on both devices,
and confirm that the watch is paired with your phone.
See Google's `Wear OS app installation help`_ for general help with installing
Wear OS apps.

.. _Wear OS app installation help: https://support.google.com/wearos/answer/7314014

Android Support
---------------

UltiObserver is Android only for now. It is designed for reasonably current Android phones.
We currently test the app on small phones, modern Pixel-style phones,
large-font settings, dark mode, and selected foldable/wide-screen checks.

If the app looks wrong or behaves badly on your phone, please let me know.
Be sure to include the following details in your report:

* phone model;
* Android version;
* whether you use large fonts, increased display size, dark mode, or
  three-button navigation;
* what you were doing when the problem appeared.


Reporting Problems
------------------

If you are able to, please use the `GitHub Issues`_ page for bug reports, rule
mistakes, confusing observer workflows, crashes, or feature requests. GitHub
issues are preferred because they keep the details in one place and make it
easier to follow up.

If GitHub is inconvenient, ping @Mike Jarvis on the observer Discord or send an
email to mikejarvis17 [at] gmail [dot] com.

.. _GitHub Issues: https://github.com/rmjarvis/UltiObserver/issues
