Installation
============

UltiObserver is currently in Google Play beta testing. To install it:

#. Join the `UltiObserver testers Google Group`_ using the same Google account
   that you use with Google Play.
#. Open the `Google Play testing page`_ and opt in to the test.
#. `Install UltiObserver from Google Play`_.

If you have trouble joining or installing, ping @Mike Jarvis on the observer
Discord, or send an email to mikejarvis17 [at] gmail [dot] com.

.. _UltiObserver testers Google Group: https://groups.google.com/g/ultiobserver-testers
.. _Google Play testing page: https://play.google.com/apps/testing/rmjarvis.ultiobserver
.. _Install UltiObserver from Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=rmjarvis.ultiobserver

Updating UltiObserver
---------------------

If you already have an earlier version installed, you do not need to uninstall
it. Make sure that you have joined the testers Google Group and opted in on the
Google Play testing page above. Google Play should then offer the latest test
version and will normally install it automatically if automatic updates are
enabled.

To install the update immediately, open these instructions on your phone, tap
`Open UltiObserver in Google Play`_, and then tap **Update**. The closed-test
listing is not searchable in the Play Store, so use this direct link to reach
it. If the button still says **Open**, check that Google Play is using the same
Google account that joined the testers group, then allow some time for the new
release to become available.

.. note::

   You can also access the Google Play link from within the app.
   Open UltiObserver's **About** page (the small circled i at the upper left
   of the home page) and tap **Check for updates**.

.. _Open UltiObserver in Google Play: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=rmjarvis.ultiobserver

If you use the watch companion, check for updates on the watch as well as the phone.
You can manage its updates through **Play Store** on the watch. If the two apps have
incompatible communication versions, the watch will identify which device needs an
update. Update that app, then tap **Retry** on the watch. Their displayed release
numbers do not have to match when their communication versions are compatible.

Installing on a Wear OS Watch
-----------------------------

UltiObserver has an optional companion app for Wear OS smart watches. If you have
one, we highly recommend also installing UltiObserver on your watch.
Install the phone app first using the instructions above, and pair your watch with
that phone using the watch manufacturer's companion app.

1. Make sure Google Play on the watch uses the same Google account that joined the
   testers group and opted into the test.
2. Open the `UltiObserver Google Play listing`_ on your phone. Use the device selector
   beside the installation controls to select your watch and install UltiObserver on it.
   The exact controls may vary with your version of Google Play.
3. Alternatively, open **Play Store** on the watch and look under **Apps on your phone**
   for UltiObserver, then tap **Install**. While we are still in closed testing, a regular
   search will not discover UltiObserver.
4. Open UltiObserver on the phone. In `Settings`, set **Watch connection** to **Wear OS**,
   then open UltiObserver on the watch.

The watch should show **No active game** until you start or resume a game on the phone.
See `Wear OS Companion App` for usage instructions and `Watch Troubleshooting` if it
does not connect.

If Google Play does not offer the watch installation, check the account on both devices,
confirm that the watch is connected, and allow time for the watch release to become
available. Installing the phone app alone does not necessarily install the watch app.
Google's `Wear OS app installation help`_ describes the available installation controls.

.. _UltiObserver Google Play listing: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=rmjarvis.ultiobserver
.. _Wear OS app installation help: https://support.google.com/wearos/answer/7314014

Android Support
---------------

UltiObserver is Android only for now. It is designed for reasonably current Android phones.
The release test matrix includes small phones, modern Pixel-style phones,
large-font settings, dark mode, and selected foldable/wide-screen checks.

If the app looks wrong or behaves badly on your phone, please report:

* phone model;
* Android version;
* whether you use large fonts, increased display size, dark mode, or
  three-button navigation;
* what you were doing when the problem appeared.


Reporting Problems
------------------

If you are able to, please use the `GitHub Issues`_ page for bug reports, rule
mistakes, confusing observer workflows, crashes, or feature requests. GitHub
issues are preferred because they keep the details in one place and make it
easier to follow up.

If GitHub is inconvenient, ping @Mike Jarvis on the observer Discord or send an
email to mikejarvis17 [at] gmail [dot] com.

.. _GitHub Issues: https://github.com/rmjarvis/UltiObserver/issues
